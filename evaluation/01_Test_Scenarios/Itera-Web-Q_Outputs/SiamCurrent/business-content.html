The Future of Green Energy in Thailand's Industrial Sector

By Somchai Prajit6 min read

Thailand is making significant strides in its transition toward renewable energy. As the government pushes for a carbon-neutral future, major industrial players are investing heavily in solar and wind infrastructure.

The recent policy shifts have made it easier for private companies to sell excess energy back to the grid, creating a new economic incentive for sustainable practices.

Experts suggest that this transition could create over 50,000 new jobs in the technology and maintenance sectors by the end of the decade.
