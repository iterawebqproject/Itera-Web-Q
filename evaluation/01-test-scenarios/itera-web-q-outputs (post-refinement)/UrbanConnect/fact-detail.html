Analysis: Digital Service Tax Claims

The Myth

Social media posts claim a 10% tax on all online shopping for consumers.

The Fact

The tax is a corporate-level adjustment for high-revenue digital providers. Consumer prices are not directly taxed.

Evidence Body

Based on the Revenue Act Section 42.A, the threshold for compliance is set at $1,000,000 SGD in annual taxable turnover.
