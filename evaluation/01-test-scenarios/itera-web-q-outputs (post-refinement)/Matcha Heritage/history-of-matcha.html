The Timeline of Tea

1191: The Arrival

The monk Eisai brings tea seeds from China to Kyoto, marking the beginning of Zen tea culture.

1500s: The Tea Ceremony

Sen no Rikyu formalizes the Way of Tea (Chado), emphasizing harmony, respect, and purity.

Modern Era

Matcha evolves from a ritualistic drink to a global superfood celebrated for its health benefits.
