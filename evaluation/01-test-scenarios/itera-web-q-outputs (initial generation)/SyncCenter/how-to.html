How to enable Two-Factor Authentication

All Plans

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account. In addition to your password, you'll need to enter a code from an app on your phone.

Instructions

  1. Go to your Profile Settings.
  2. Select the 'Security' tab.
  3. Click 'Enable 2FA'.
  4. Scan the QR code with your preferred authenticator app.
